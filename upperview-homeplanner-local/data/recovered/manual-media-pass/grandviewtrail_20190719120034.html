HOME OWNERS FIRST. BUILT TO LAST.

For decades, the Chan and Tucciarone families have been building residential subdivision and custom homes throughout the Greater Toronto Area. The vast business and construction experience/wisdom accumulated by the first generation has been passed down to their children. Equipped with the old and new school methods of business and construction, the second generation families have formed a partnership dedicated to build quality homes that withstand the test of time. “Home Owners First, Built To Last” is the foundation of Upperview Homes’ philosophy. We carry this philosophy as our mandate to provide our potential home owners with the best new home buying experience from walking into our sales centres to the move-in day to the worry free warranty period and beyond. We understand that our homes shelter the lives and memories of those living within. As a family-owned and operated business, we feel privileged to have a hand in the vibrant communities where families work, play and grow together.

Our dedication to quality is evident in the individual care and detail we apply to each stage of the home building process, using only the finest materials and craftsmanship. We work with trades and suppliers who share our business philosophy and as a result, the Upperview Homes name has become synonymous with quality and superior workmanship.

Upperview Homes is building the home of your dreams, we welcome you to become a part of our tradition. To learn more about Upperview Homes, visit the website at upperviewhomes.com
